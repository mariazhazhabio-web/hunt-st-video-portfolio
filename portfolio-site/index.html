Project 01 · Luxury residence showcase

A walkthrough timed to the piano

An inspection-order tour of one modern house: arrival, entrance, living, kitchen, bedroom, ensuite, view, outdoor living, highlights, and a brand close. Every cut lands on the music's beat.

Editing decisions

  • Beat-grid pacing. Hero wides hold 6 beats, supporting shots 4, details 2, so the rhythm tightens for kitchen and bathroom details.
  • Frame-rate conform. 30 fps footage plays at 83% on a 25 fps timeline for a calmer, gliding feel without stutter.
  • Transitions with a reason. Hard cuts on the beat, with dissolves only at chapter changes (living, bedroom, highlights).
  • Digital push-ins of 3–5% on static-feeling shots to keep the camera alive.

Look and type

  • Warm-neutral grade: +6% contrast, −10% saturation, warm highlights, cool shadows, soft vignette.
  • Room captions in serif italic, lower left inside title-safe, over a soft gradient so they read on bright windows.
  • Brand end card with call to action, editor credit and sample disclaimer.

Process

From brief to delivered file

  1. 01 · Brief

    Plan the story

    Creative brief, audience, structure, then a storyboard and a timed shot list with framing, camera move, speed, transition and on-screen text for every shot.

  2. 02 · Source

    License everything

    Footage from Pexels and music from Pixabay, logged with creator and link. Tracks registered with YouTube Content ID were filtered out so the samples can be posted without copyright claims.

  3. 03 · Cut

    Edit to the music

    Measure the track's tempo, choose the section that fits the story, and set every shot length to whole beats. Then grade, add titles and transitions, and mix to −14 LUFS.

  4. 04 · QC

    Check before delivery

    Frame count and duration, resolution and frame rate, loudness and true peak, black-frame and silence detection, title-safe and Instagram safe zones, and a disclaimer on every end card.
